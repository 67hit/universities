# What consulting firms in Europe rely on game theory in their practice?

Several consulting firms in Europe use **game theory** as a core part of their strategic advisory services, particularly in areas like **competitive strategy**, **negotiations**, **pricing**, **market design**, and **regulatory strategy**. Here's a list of firms (or teams within larger firms) that are known for this:

---

### 🔹 **TWS Partners** (Germany-based, pan-European)

* **Headquarters:** Munich, Germany
* **Specialty:** TWS Partners is *the* leading consulting firm in Europe that explicitly builds its entire consulting methodology around **game theory and market design**.
* **Focus areas:** Procurement, negotiations, pricing, regulatory strategy, M\&A, and auction design.
* **Clients:** Large corporates across sectors including automotive, energy, pharmaceuticals, and telecommunications.
* **Offices:** Germany, UK, Austria, Switzerland, Netherlands.

---

### 🔹 **Tilt (formerly STR Applied Game Theory)** – UK

* **Headquarters:** London, UK
* **Background:** Small consultancy specializing in using game theory and behavioral science in strategic decision-making.
* **Focus areas:** Pricing, negotiation strategy, and competitive dynamics.
* **Note:** Founded by academics and former strategy consultants.

---

### 🔹 **Bain & Company – Advanced Analytics and Strategy Group**

* **Europe-wide presence**
* **Application:** Uses game theory tools in **competitive strategy, M\&A, and market simulations**.
* While not a game-theory-focused firm per se, they incorporate **game-theoretic modeling** especially in projects involving pricing wars, auction design, or competitor analysis.

---

### 🔹 **McKinsey & Company – Strategy & Advanced Analytics**

* **Europe-wide, esp. Germany and UK**
* McKinsey often uses game theory in **scenario planning** and **strategic simulations** (e.g., war gaming), particularly in high-stakes markets like telecom, pharma, and energy.

---

### 🔹 **The LSP Group (London Strategy Partners)** – UK

* Boutique strategy consultancy with strong academic roots and history of **applying game-theoretic thinking** in pricing and competitive strategy.

---

### 🔹 **OXERA** – UK-based, with offices across Europe

* **Focus:** Economics and policy consulting.
* **Application:** Uses game theory in **regulatory economics**, **competition cases**, and **auction design**, especially in energy and telecoms.
* **Clients:** European Commission, OFCOM, major corporates.

---

### 🔹 **Compass Lexecon** – Europe & US

* **Application:** In **competition economics** and litigation support, especially for M\&A and antitrust work.
* **Game theory usage:** Formal modeling of strategic firm behavior under different regulatory or market scenarios.

---

### 🔹 **Charles River Associates (CRA International)** – UK and EU offices

* **Focus:** Game theory applied to **competition and litigation consulting**, especially for regulators and antitrust authorities.
* **Key areas:** Merger simulations, collusion modeling, and auction strategy.
