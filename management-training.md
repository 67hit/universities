# What is the average hourly price of management consulting in Hungary?

The **average hourly price for management consulting in Hungary** varies a lot depending on whether you look at **freelancers, boutique firms, or large consulting firms**. But typical market ranges can be summarized fairly well.

## 1. Freelance / Independent Consultants

* Roughly **€10–€50 per hour** in Budapest for many freelance consulting engagements. ([faruse.com][1])
* Junior or less specialized consultants often fall near **€10–€25/hour**.
* Experienced consultants or niche experts may charge **€40–€50/hour** or more.

➡️ **Typical freelance average:** **€25–€40/hour**

## 2. Eastern European Consulting Market Benchmarks

A global freelance rate index suggests that **senior business or management consultants in Eastern Europe** average around **$95/hour (~€85/hour)**, with experienced consultants ranging higher. ([Jobbers][2])

➡️ **Typical regional consulting benchmark:** **€70–€120/hour**

## 3. Large Consulting Firms (Implied Billable Rates)

Employee salaries in Hungary imply lower internal costs but higher client billing rates:

* A management consultant salary in Hungary averages around **HUF ~670k/month equivalent**, with hourly compensation roughly **HUF 323/hour (~€0.8)** for the employee. ([Glassdoor][3])

However, consulting firms typically bill **3–6× employee cost**, meaning client-facing rates often land around:

➡️ **Large firm billable rate estimate:** **€80–€200/hour**

## 4. Typical Market Segments

| Type of consultant                             | Typical hourly rate |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |

| Experienced freelancer                         | €25–€60             |
| Boutique consulting firm                       | €60–€120            |

**≈ €40–€120 per hour**, depending on experience and firm type.
